Corporate Housing Services

Our Corporate Housing Services provide flexible and customized accommodation solutions for relocating employees and their families. Designed to support global mobility programs, we ensure assignees are placed in comfortable, compliant, and well-located housing that aligns with corporate budgets and assignment requirements.

Key Offerings:

  • Short-term & long-term housing solutions: Flexible accommodation options tailored for short-term projects, long-term assignments, and extended stays, supporting varying assignment durations and business needs.
  • Fully furnished apartments & serviced residences: Access to fully furnished apartments and serviced residences equipped with essential amenities, allowing assignees to move in immediately without setup delays.
  • Extended-stay hotel arrangements: Coordinated extended-stay hotel solutions for temporary housing needs, ensuring comfort, convenience, and proximity to business locations.
  • Customized housing based on employee needs: Personalized accommodation selection based on family size, lifestyle preferences, location requirements, and corporate housing policies.
  • Budget-aligned housing management: Housing solutions structured to align with corporate cost controls while maintaining quality standards and employee comfort.
  • End-to-end housing coordination: Complete management from property sourcing and booking to move-in support and transition handling, ensuring a smooth and stress-free settling-in experience.
