Destination Service

Kraft Mobility ensures a smooth transition to the relocating country with tailored services for employees and their families. From preview trips to home search and settling-in support, we plan every detail with care. Our approach is personalized, thoughtful, and focused on the unique needs of each family. We make international relocation comfortable, seamless, and stress-free.

Orientation program

Our relocation consultants create a detailed itinerary tailored to the priorities of employees and their families. The plan covers all key aspects, including sample housing visits, schooling options, recreational activities, shopping, and healthcare. Every detail is carefully considered to provide a comprehensive overview of the new location. This ensures a smooth, informed, and confident transition for the relocating family.

Home Search

Our aim is to find the perfect home for each relocating family based on their needs and preferences. With deep local knowledge and a strong partner network, we leave no stone unturned in the search for the ideal property. From the initial consultation and property shortlisting to lease negotiations and preparing the home for arrival, every step is managed with precision. We ensure a seamless, stress-free experience that helps families settle in comfortably.

School Search

We provide detailed information and research on both local and international schools to support relocating families. Our team shares comprehensive listings that include curriculum options, facilities, locations, admission procedures, and fee structures. This ensures employees have all the information they need to make informed decisions. We simplify the school search process, helping families find the right educational fit with ease.

Settling-in

We offer a range of settling-in services, including assistance with opening bank accounts, setting up internet and mobile connections, and arranging utilities. Our support ensures that every essential task is handled smoothly and efficiently. By managing these details, we make the relocation experience seamless and stress-free. Families can focus on settling into their new environment with confidence and ease.

Departure Support

Our departure support program ensures that moving out from the current home is smooth and stress-free. We assist with disconnecting utilities, closing bank accounts, coordinating repairs, and managing the return of security deposits. Every step is handled efficiently to minimize hassle and ensure a seamless transition. Families can focus on their next destination with peace of mind.

Tenancy management

Our comprehensive tenancy management services ensure a smooth and hassle-free stay for expatriates and their families. With a single point of contact, expats don’t need to coordinate with multiple service providers. From plumbing and electrical issues to internet, gas, mobile, or any other household concern, our team handles everything efficiently. We make living abroad convenient, comfortable, and worry-free.
