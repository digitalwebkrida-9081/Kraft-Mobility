HYDERABAD

General Overview

Hyderabad is the capital city of Telangana, located in south-central India along the Musi River. The city sits on the Deccan Plateau at an average elevation of 542 meters (1,778 feet) above sea level. It is known for its rocky terrain, granite formations, and a unique blend of historical and modern urban landscapes.

Area: Approximately 650 Square Kilometres

Founded in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah of the Qutb Shahi dynasty, Hyderabad has grown into a major metropolitan region along with Secunderabad and Cyberabad. The city is widely known as the “City of Pearls” and is a leading pharmaceutical and IT hub of India.

Climate

Summer: March–June (Up to 42°C)

Monsoon: June–September (Moderate Rainfall)

Winter: November–February (12°C–25°C)

Best Time: October to February

Population: ~10.8 Million

Languages: Telugu, Urdu, Hindi, English

Literacy: 83%

Housing Overview

Neighbourhoods & Areas

Hyderabad’s rental market has grown steadily over the last decade, driven largely by its booming IT and business ecosystem. With major technology hubs such as HITEC City, Gachibowli, Nanakramguda, and the Financial District, demand for quality housing—particularly in western Hyderabad— has increased significantly.

While central Hyderabad features older neighbourhoods and heritage-style buildings, western Hyderabad offers modern gated communities, high-rise apartments, and villa developments that are preferred by expatriates and corporate professionals. Although public transport options include metro rail, buses, and auto rickshaws, expats generally prefer personal vehicles or chauffeur-driven cabs due to comfort and convenience.

Preferred residential areas include Gachibowli, HITEC City, Kondapur, Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Madhapur, Manikonda, Puppalaguda, Kokapet, Narsingi . These localities offer superior infrastructure, proximity to IT hubs, international schools, hospitals, and modern lifestyle amenities.

Housing Options

Temporary Accommodation

Hyderabad offers a wide range of premium hotels and serviced residences for short-term stays, especially in the IT corridor and central areas. Advance booking is recommended during peak business seasons, major conferences, and festival periods such as Dussehra and Diwali.

Popular options include ITC Kohenur, Trident Hyderabad, Taj Krishna, Hyatt Hyderabad Gachibowli, The Westin Hyderabad Mindspace, and Novotel Hyderabad Convention Centre.

Long Term Accommodation

Long-term housing options in Hyderabad include independent houses, villas, and high-rise apartments. Gated communities—particularly in Gachibowli, Nanakramguda, Kokapet, and Kondapur—offer enhanced security, power backup, clubhouse facilities, swimming pools, gyms, landscaped gardens, and in-house maintenance services.

Independent houses, mainly found in Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, and Begumpet, provide more space and privacy but may require private security and additional maintenance arrangements.

Villa communities are commonly located in Narsingi, Kokapet, Tellapur, and parts of Gachibowli, offering premium living with community-level amenities.

Healthcare

Prevention and Immunisation

Health Risks

Common health risks in Hyderabad include dengue fever, malaria, chikungunya, typhoid, hepatitis A & B, cholera, diarrhoeal diseases, and seasonal influenza. During the monsoon season, mosquito-borne illnesses increase, particularly in low-lying or waterlogged areas.

Air quality in Hyderabad can fluctuate due to traffic congestion and industrial activity, which may impact individuals with asthma, COPD, or other respiratory conditions.

It is strongly advised to ensure that all routine vaccinations are up-to-date before arrival, especially for families and long-term residents.

Recommended Vaccinations for India

  • Ensure routine immunisations such as influenza, chickenpox (varicella), polio, MMR, and DPT are current for all age groups.
  • Hepatitis A: Recommended for all travellers above 1 year of age. Administer at least 2 weeks before travel, with a booster after 6–12 months.
  • Typhoid: Available as oral capsules (4 doses, age 6+) or a single injectable dose (age 2+).
  • Polio: Adults without a booster should receive one inactivated polio dose; children must be fully immunised.
  • Hepatitis B: Recommended for long-term visitors, individuals with close community contact, or those likely to require medical care. Given as a standard 3-dose series (0, 1, 6 months) or an accelerated schedule if needed.

Medical Emergency Checklist

In a medical emergency, knowing the nearest hospital is essential. Hyderabad’s traffic and expanding urban layout can sometimes delay emergency response times.

Many expatriates prefer using private taxis or ride-hailing services such as Uber or Ola for faster transport to hospitals, unless the patient is immobile.

Always carry personal identification such as your passport, visa, or Residential Permit (RP). Private hospitals may require advance payment at the time of admission.

Schooling Overview

Hyderabad Schools

Education in Hyderabad is available from Kindergarten through Higher Education. Early education follows the kindergarten model, while primary and secondary education is governed by multiple national and international education boards.

The Telangana State Board of Secondary Education (TSBSE) conducts the SSC (Class 10) examinations, while Higher Secondary education (Classes 11 and 12) prepares students for university and professional courses.

Other prominent boards present in Hyderabad include CBSE, CISCE (ICSE/ISC), NIOS, Cambridge (IGCSE), and the International Baccalaureate (IB) .

School Categories

  • Government Schools (fully funded and run by the state)
  • Aided Schools (partially funded by the government)
  • Unaided Private Schools (independently managed with modern facilities and higher fees)

After completing secondary education, students move to Higher Secondary (Grades 11 and 12) or Pre-University Courses (PUC), enabling entry into colleges, universities, and professional institutions. Hyderabad also hosts numerous reputed higher education and research institutions.

International Schools in Hyderabad

Hyderabad’s growth as a global IT and business hub has resulted in a wide range of international schools catering to expatriates, NRIs, and globally mobile families.

HHG Shipment Overview

Household Goods Shipment – Hyderabad

Indian nationals and foreign nationals, including persons of Indian origin, transferring their residence to Hyderabad or arriving in India on employment are permitted to import personal effects and household goods into India duty-free, subject to specific eligibility conditions and customs regulations.

Eligibility & Visa Requirements

For Indian nationals, the following criteria must be met:

  • Must have lived overseas for at least two years and be transferring residence to India
  • Must not have visited India for more than 180 days in the preceding two years

For foreign nationals, the following conditions apply:

  • Must hold a valid Work / Employment Visa with a minimum validity of one year
  • Mandatory registration with the Foreigners Regional Registration Office (FRRO) and issuance of a Residential Permit
  • The original passport must be presented at the time of customs clearance

Shipment Timelines

  • Air shipments: Must be shipped within 15 days of the owner’s arrival in India
  • Sea shipments: Must be shipped within 30 days of the owner’s arrival
  • Cars: Can be shipped within six months of arrival in India

Shipments delayed beyond the above timelines may be considered for customs clearance only on a case-by-case basis, subject to customs approval.

It is essential that the owner arrives in India before the shipment. Failure to do so may result in significant demurrage or container detention charges.

Shipments bound for Hyderabad are generally cleared at Hyderabad Airport (air cargo) or Mumbai Port (JNPT) for sea cargo, followed by inland transportation to Hyderabad.

The earlier requirement of a compulsory one-year stay in India after availing Transfer of Residence (TR) benefits has been removed. Importers may leave India after claiming TR concessions.

Household Goods – Duty Structure

Duty-Free Imports

Old and used personal effects and household goods are permitted duty-free, provided they have been used by the shipper and the individual holds a valid one-year visa.

Examples include:

  • Clothes and personal linen
  • Books and personal documents
  • Kitchenware and utensils
  • Furniture
  • Small household appliances (mixer, juicer, iron, etc.)

Concessional Duty at 15%

The following items are allowed at a concessional duty rate of 15% for one unit of each item, subject to a combined value limit of ₹5,00,000 (approx. USD 10,000), regardless of usage:

  • Television (TV)
  • DVD Player
  • Video Home Theatre System
  • Dishwasher
  • Music System
  • Air Conditioner
  • Deep Freezer
  • Microwave Oven
  • Video Camera
  • Word Processor
  • Fax Machine
  • Portable Photocopier

Duty-Free Appliances

  • Laptop or Desktop Computer
  • Refrigerator (up to 300 litres)
  • Washing Machine
  • Cooking Range
  • VCR / VCD Player

One of the most active global networks is the InterNations – Hyderabad Chapter, which hosts regular meetups, social evenings, coffee mornings, and interest-based gatherings. These events are ideal for networking, making friends, and sharing practical advice.

Shopping Overview

Shopping in Hyderabad is a rich and diverse experience, blending centuries-old bazaars with modern malls and global retail brands. Known for its heritage, craftsmanship, and vibrant street life, the city caters equally to bargain hunters, culture enthusiasts, and luxury shoppers.

As a historic trading center and a modern IT metropolis, Hyderabad offers everything from traditional pearls, bangles, and textiles to international fashion, electronics, and lifestyle products. Whether you prefer lively street markets or air-conditioned malls, the city has something for every taste and budget.

Shopping districts in Hyderabad range from heritage markets clustered around the Old City to contemporary retail hubs in western Hyderabad.

Traditional Markets & Street Shopping

Laad Bazaar / Charminar Area

One of Hyderabad’s most iconic markets, Laad Bazaar is famous for its lacquer bangles, bridal wear, pearls, zari work, and traditional textiles. Dating back to the Qutb Shahi and Nizam eras, this bustling market is a living heritage zone. Bargaining is essential, and prices can be surprisingly low once negotiated. The area also offers rich street food and is best visited in the late afternoon or evening.

Charminar & Old City Bazaars

The streets surrounding Charminar offer a sensory shopping experience filled with textiles, jewelry, spices, perfumes, souvenirs, and local snacks. This area is ideal for authentic cultural shopping, traditional crafts, and Hyderabad’s famous pearl trade.

Begum Bazaar

One of the oldest and largest wholesale markets in Hyderabad, Begum Bazaar is known for spices, dry fruits, incense, copper and steel utensils, and puja items. It is ideal for stocking household essentials at wholesale prices and offers a strong local character with plenty of bargaining opportunities.

Koti / Sultan Bazaar

A lively and budget-friendly market popular with students and young shoppers, offering trendy clothing, silver jewelry, cosmetics, handbags, stationery, books, and fabrics. It’s a great spot for quick fashion finds and affordable shopping.

Shilparamam (HITEC City)

A cultural crafts village showcasing handmade textiles, tribal jewelry, terracotta items, brass décor, woodwork, and folk art. Shilparamam regularly hosts seasonal fairs, cultural performances, and craft exhibitions—making it a pleasant mix of shopping, entertainment, and cultural exploration.

Moazzam Jahi / Flower & General Bazaars

Traditional local markets offering flowers, fruits, and daily essentials in heritage surroundings. These markets are excellent for experiencing everyday local life, casual street shopping, photography, and sampling quick local snacks.

Shopping Malls & Modern Retail

Inorbit Mall, Madhapur

A large and well-known mall in Hyderabad’s IT hub, featuring international fashion brands, beauty stores, children’s outlets, a multiplex cinema, gaming zones, and a wide range of dining options. Easily accessible by metro and suitable for families and professionals alike.

Lulu Mall, Kukatpally

A popular modern shopping destination offering a broad mix of Indian and international brands, electronics, and a large food court. Ideal for casual shopping and relaxed dining in a spacious environment.

GVK One, Banjara Hills

An upscale mall known for premium brands, elegant interiors with a central aquarium, and entertainment options such as INOX cinemas and gaming arcades. Appeals to fashion-forward shoppers and families seeking a luxury experience.

City Center Mall, Banjara Hills

A family-friendly mall with multiple floors of retail outlets, specialty stores, gaming zones, and diverse dining options. Its central location and metro connectivity make it convenient for frequent visits.

Hyderabad Central, Punjagutta

A five-storey retail complex offering fashion, accessories, jewelry, footwear, a multiplex cinema, and regular promotional events. Well-connected by metro and ideal for combining shopping and entertainment.

Nexus Hyderabad Mall, Kukatpally

A large retail and entertainment complex near major residential and commercial areas. It features a wide brand mix, hypermarket, family entertainment centers such as Smaaash, and attractions suitable for full-day family outings.

Hyderabad’s shopping scene seamlessly combines heritage markets and modern retail spaces, giving residents and expatriates the flexibility to choose between traditional craftsmanship, everyday essentials, and contemporary global brands—all within one dynamic city.
